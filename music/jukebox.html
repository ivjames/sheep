🐑 Come By! Jukebox

Every track below is CC BY 4.0 music — attribution required, unlike the CC0 rounds — by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com) and Jason Shaw (audionautix.com). This is the upbeat-guitar round. The files live in this folder as loudness-normalized MP3s. Whichever tracks get wired into the game must be credited — the exact credit lines live in music/SOURCES.md. Audition each one, toggle keep on the tracks that belong in the game, then copy the pick list at the bottom and paste it back to Claude to wire the music into the game. The four badged tracks now play in the game as a shuffled background playlist. A fresh round-4 section of banjo/bluegrass candidates awaits audition at the bottom.
