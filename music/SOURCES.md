# Music sources

All tracks are **CC0 (public domain)** from [OpenGameArt.org](https://opengameart.org),
found via the advanced search with the `CC0` license and `Music` filters. Searches included
genre/mood terms like "celtic", "pastoral", "meadow", "village theme", "harp", and "folk flute",
plus a comprehensive sweep through RandomMind's medieval music set. CC0 requires no attribution,
but composers are credited below anyway. FreePD.com, the other major CC0 music library, shut
down in 2026, making OpenGameArt the primary source for this collection. The files in this
folder start from the source download (MP3/OGG/WAV/FLAC originals), get loudness-normalized
to −16 LUFS (EBU R128, true peak −1.5 dB) so they audition at matched volume, and are
re-encoded 44.1 kHz VBR MP3 (~100 kbps). For final in-game use the originals can be re-fetched
at full quality from the linked pages.

None are wired into the game yet — audition in `music/jukebox.html`, then tell Claude which to wire in. Loop-ready versions of some RandomMind tracks (The Bard's Tale, Market Day, Rejoicing) exist on their source pages; Spring's Pastorales III & IV are further options on the same page as I & II.
