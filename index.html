Come By!

A border collie. A restless flock. One open gate.

You're the sheepdog. Sheep drift away from you — use that pressure to steer the flock.
Push every sheep through the gate into the pen to clear the level.
Defiant sheep barely care about you. They plant their hooves and stray from the flock. They don't bite — they're just stubborn.
Charge right at a sheep and the dog barks — a burst of pressure, and the only thing that moves a planted sheep.
Prefer real trial handling? In command mode the dog works alone and you call it: come by (clockwise), away (counter), walk on (faster each tap), easy (slower), steady (halt), to me (call off), that'll do.

Click to send the dog (or WASD / arrows) · charge a sheep to bark · 1–7 call commands · swap modes any time
